Getting started

Installing Hushpad

Opening it the first time

  1. Open Hushpad.dmg and drag Hushpad into Applications.
  2. Open Hushpad. When macOS says it can't verify it, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter your Mac password. Hushpad opens, and you won't see this again.

Hushpad is made by a small independent developer, so macOS asks you to approve it once.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)
  • macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • About 6 GB free. Hushpad downloads its AI models (about 5.3 GB) once, on first launch.
  • 16 GB of memory recommended
  • Works in English
Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an internet connection?

Only once, to download the AI models during setup. After that, dictation works completely offline.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

Hushpad only listens while you hold your key. Recordings stay on your Mac, encrypted, so you can replay or redo a dictation. They're never uploaded.

How is this different from the Mac's built-in dictation?

Hushpad is more accurate, cleans up your speech, formats lists, times and money, lets you edit text with your voice, learns your names, and keeps a full history of everything you've said.

What happens after the free trial?

After 7 days, Hushpad asks for a license key. Buy one on Gumroad, paste it in, and you're set. Nothing you dictated during the trial is lost.
