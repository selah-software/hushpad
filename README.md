# Hushpad

Private, on-device dictation for Mac. Hold a key, talk, let go, and clean, punctuated text appears in any app. Speech recognition and the AI cleanup run entirely on your Mac, so your voice is never uploaded.

**Website:** https://selah-software.github.io/hushpad/

## Try it free

[Download Hushpad](https://github.com/selah-software/hushpad/releases/latest/download/Hushpad.dmg) and use every feature free for 7 days. No account needed.

## Keep using it

Buy a license at **https://selahsoftware.gumroad.com/l/hushpad**. No monthly subscription. Then open Hushpad, go to its License screen, and paste your key. One license works on up to 2 Macs.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or later
- About 6 GB of free space for the on-device models (downloaded on first launch)
- English

The first time you open Hushpad, macOS may say it can't check it for malicious software. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.
