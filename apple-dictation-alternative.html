Apple's built-in dictation types what it hears, mistakes and all. Hushpad gives you the same hold-a-key-and-talk workflow, then cleans the text up before it appears: no filler words, proper punctuation, formatted lists, and your names spelled right.

What Hushpad does

  • Removes “um”s, repeats and false starts, and fixes punctuation and capitals.
  • Formats lists, times, money and email addresses the way you meant them.
  • Handles changes of mind: “meet at 3, no wait, 4” becomes “Meet at 4.”
  • Lets you edit highlighted text by voice: “make this more polite,” “shorten this.”
  • Learns names you correct, and inserts snippets like “my signature.”
  • Keeps an encrypted, searchable history of everything you've dictated.

What you need

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 13 or later, about 6 GB of free space for the on-device AI models, and 16 GB of memory recommended. Hushpad works in English.
