Private dictation

Dictation for Mac that never uploads your voice

Many AI dictation apps send your recordings to a server to be transcribed. Hushpad doesn't. Speech recognition (Whisper) and the AI that cleans up your text both run on your own Mac, so what you say stays on your computer.

What goes over the internet

Only two things: checking your license key, and checking for app updates. Nothing you say or write is ever sent.

Who it's for

Anyone who writes a lot and cares where their words go: people handling client or patient details, writers, pastors and ministry staff, and anyone who'd rather not have a recording of their voice on someone else's server.

What Hushpad does

  • Removes “um”s, repeats and false starts, and fixes punctuation and capitals.
  • Formats lists, times, money and email addresses the way you meant them.
  • Handles changes of mind: “meet at 3, no wait, 4” becomes “Meet at 4.”
  • Lets you edit highlighted text by voice: “make this more polite,” “shorten this.”
  • Learns names you correct, and inserts snippets like “my signature.”
  • Keeps an encrypted, searchable history of everything you've dictated.

What you need

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 13 or later, about 6 GB of free space for the on-device AI models, and 16 GB of memory recommended. Hushpad works in English.
